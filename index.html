ORGANELLE DYNAMICS & SIGNALING

How do organelle dynamics shape cell signaling and metabolic function?

We investigate how centrosomes and primary cilia change during cell differentiation and how these changes influence signaling and cellular function, with a particular focus on adipocyte biology.

RESEARCH

All projects →

Centrosome & cilium dynamics

We study how centrosomes and primary cilia are assembled, maintained, and remodeled as cells differentiate, and how their organization contributes to cell function.

Adipocyte biology & metabolism

We explore how organelle dynamics contribute to adipocyte differentiation and metabolic function, including lipid storage and insulin responsiveness.

Cellular signaling

We investigate how centrosomes and primary cilia organize signaling pathways, linking changes in organelle structure to cellular responses.
