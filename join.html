JOIN US

We are building the team

We welcome inquiries from motivated undergraduate and prospective master’s students interested in organelle biology, cellular signaling, and metabolism. We value curiosity, careful experimentation, and learning together—and aim to make the lab a welcoming, supportive, and fun place to do science.

STUDENT RESEARCH OPPORTUNITIES

MSc Students

We welcome prospective master’s students interested in studying centrosomes, primary cilia, and their roles in cellular differentiation and disease. Please contact us to discuss potential research projects and graduate program options. Admission and any financial support are subject to the relevant university requirements and funding availability.

Undergraduates and interns

We welcome undergraduate students interested in gaining research experience in molecular and cell biology. Please get in touch to discuss opportunities, your availability, and how your interests align with our research.

HOW TO APPLY

Please email melis.arslanhan@medipol.edu.tr with:

  • Your CV, including your current program and year of study.
  • A short introduction explaining your research interests and why you would like to join the lab.
  • Your intended start date and availability.
